STAGES OF CREATION

Process

  • The Genesis

    Conceptualization begins in silence. A meditative period where charcoal sketches capture the raw geometry of a feeling before any physical form is considered.

  • Material Dialogue

    Selecting the earth. Alina works with rare clays and volcanic minerals, seeking a texture that speaks of geological time and human vulnerability.

  • The Transmutation

    The kiln firing is the final collaborator. In the intense heat, the piece finds its permanent character, revealing cracks and hues that are beyond the artist's control.

What is your availability for commissions?

Alina's availability for commissions varies based on her current schedule and project commitments. Please reach out through the contact form to discuss potential opportunities and timelines.

What is your return policy?

Due to the nature of handmade ceramic pieces, all sales are final. Please ensure you are satisfied with the piece before making a purchase.

How do I care for my ceramic piece?

Handmade ceramic pieces should be handled with care. Avoid exposing them to extreme temperatures or direct sunlight. Clean them gently with a soft, dry cloth.

What is your shipping policy?

Shipping costs and delivery times may vary based on your location and the size of your order. Please contact us for more information.

Which pieces are available for purchase?

We have a variety of handmade ceramic pieces available for purchase at any given time. Please check back regularly as new pieces are added.
